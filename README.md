# Which Dimension?

A scrolling, animated explainer about the different things people mean by "dimension".

- **Axial:** three spatial measures, and time as the progression between sets of locations.
- **Parallel:** branching causalities, and different positions on one timeline.
- **Scalar:** other causalities sharing our space and time, separated only by orders of magnitude of size.
- **Frequential:** layers of matter that share our space but never interact, except possibly through gravity.

One file, no dependencies. Open `index.html` in a browser and scroll.

Live: https://sh0tybumbati.github.io/dimensions/

The parallel, scalar and frequential chapters are speculative framings, and the page tags them as such.
